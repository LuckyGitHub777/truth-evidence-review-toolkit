A claim under review.

INVENTED STATEMENT“A fictional town's public fountain ran continuously for a year without any maintenance.”

01 · What exactly is claimed?

Define measurable terms: continuous, one year and no maintenance. Each has a separate evidentiary burden.

02 · What source could test it?

Contemporaneous logs, authentic records and independent observations—not a repeated headline.

03 · What remains unknown?

No source documents have been supplied to this fictional case. Missing evidence cannot be filled with confidence.

04 · What is safe to publish?

A neutral status note describing uncertainty, not an unsourced factual assertion.
