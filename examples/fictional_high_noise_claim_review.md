# Fictional High-Noise Claim Review

## Scenario

A fictional social media post claims that a new AI crisis-response system is secretly making military escalation decisions without human review.

## Claim extraction

| Claim ID | Exact Claim | Evidence Level | Risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EX-001 | A specific AI system exists. | Speculative | Medium | RESEARCH-BACKLOG |
| EX-002 | The AI system makes escalation decisions. | Unsupported | Severe | DO-NOT-PUBLISH |
| EX-003 | Human review is bypassed. | Unsupported | Severe | DO-NOT-PUBLISH |
| EX-004 | The claim should be reviewed before sharing. | Established | Low | PUBLIC-OK |

## Public-safe statement

> A claim is circulating about an alleged AI crisis-response system. We have not verified the claim. Before drawing conclusions, the exact system, source, decision authority, and human-review process would need to be independently documented.

## Lesson

Do not amplify high-risk claims before evidence review.
